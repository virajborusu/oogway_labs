Title: Elena Verna on Product-Led Growth, Product-Led Sales, and Freemium Models
Guest: Elena Verna
Source: Lenny's Podcast — Episode 65

Elena Verna (Growth Advisor to Miro, Amplitude, Dropbox, Netlify) shares insights on modern PLG strategies, monetization timing, and bridging product usage with enterprise sales.

### What Product-Led Growth (PLG) Really Means
PLG is an organizational strategy where product usage is the primary driver of customer acquisition, retention, and expansion.
- **Freemium / Free Trial**: Give users immediate value without friction before asking for credit card information.
- **Time-to-Value (TTV)**: Minimize the minutes it takes for a new sign-up to reach their first "Aha!" moment.

### Product-Led Sales (PLS)
Product-led growth does not replace enterprise sales; it fuels enterprise sales.
1. Self-serve users sign up and use the product inside an organization.
2. Product telemetry identifies "Product Qualified Leads" (PQLs)—teams with high active usage, team collaboration, and feature adoption.
3. Account executives reach out to corporate leadership with usage evidence, transforming cold sales pitches into warm account expansions.
